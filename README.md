### About me

I'm a French entrepreneur based in Dubai. I've been building in crypto and tech since 2020 — co-founded [Le Crypto Daily](https://lecryptodaily.com) (largest francophone crypto media), launched a SocialFi app that raised $1.5M and debuted at CES Las Vegas, and took an operational role at [Chainraizer](https://chainraizer-docs.vercel.app) applying blockchain to private equity tokenization.

Today I focus on algorithmic asset management at ARTE One, where I work on AI-driven trading systems and quantitative strategies.

I believe technology is a form of art that raises moral questions in its application. I build things that sit at the edge of what's possible.
