# Oscar Mairey

Entrepreneur, builder, deep tech. Currently an algorithmic asset manager at [ARTE One](https://github.com/arte-group), working at the intersection of AI and quantitative finance.

Previously took an operational role at [Chainraizer](https://chainraizer-docs.vercel.app), applying blockchain to traditional finance through private equity tokenization. Before that, co-founded [Le Crypto Daily](https://lecryptodaily.com) — the largest francophone crypto media — and launched a SocialFi app that raised $1.5M and debuted at CES Las Vegas.

## What I build

I work across the full stack with a focus on TypeScript, React/Next.js, Web3, and machine learning. Most of my work sits at the boundary between finance, AI, and blockchain.

**Current focus:** algorithmic trading systems, AI-driven asset management, and tokenized finance infrastructure.
